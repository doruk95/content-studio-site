Content Studio

Content Studio is a private tool that the owner of the YouTube channels Fact Me Up! and Şaşırt Beni! runs on their own computer. It helps make short videos about curious facts, in English and Turkish.

Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is uploaded. The tool then uploads it to the owner's own channel and shows the owner their own channel statistics.

Content Studio is not offered to anyone else: there are no user accounts, no sign-ups and no public service.
