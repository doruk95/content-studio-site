Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

Content Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Fact Me Up! and Şaşırt Beni! on their own computer. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Google data the tool uses

  • Upload access (youtube.upload): to upload videos the owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's own channels.
  • Read-only YouTube access (youtube.readonly): to confirm the connected channel is the right one and to find videos it already uploaded.
  • Read-only YouTube Analytics access (yt-analytics.readonly): to show the owner the statistics of their own videos.

How it is stored

The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored encrypted, only on the owner's computer. Video statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is stored on any server run by Content Studio; there is none. The tool stores and reads this information only in local files on the owner's computer. This website sets no cookies and has no tracking.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared or sent anywhere except to Google's own APIs to perform the actions above.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all uploads and statistics. To have locally stored data deleted, write to the contact address below; it is deleted within 30 days.
